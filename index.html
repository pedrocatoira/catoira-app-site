Approach · Agile Product Management

Ship small. Learn fast.
Scale what works.

Big plans built on assumptions are the most expensive way to make a product. Every engagement — coaching or building — follows the same loop: put something real in front of a few customers, learn from it, and only invest further when the evidence says so.

  1. 01

    Test with a few customers

    A prototype or thin MVP in the hands of a small group, aimed at the riskiest assumption.

  2. 02

    Ship

    Release early, in small increments, so real usage starts as soon as possible.

  3. 03

    Gather feedback

    Usage data and direct conversations with users — what they do, not only what they say.

  4. 04

    Change

    Adjust the product, the plan or the process based on what was learned — then loop again.

  5. 05

    Scale when needed

    Invest in growth, infrastructure and teams once the signal is clear — not before.

Steps 1–4 repeat as often as needed. Each release is a chance to learn something.
